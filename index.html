Duff Omacal

Duff Omacal is a personal calendar widget for the desktop bar of an Omarchy Linux computer. It shows the owner's Google Calendar events and lets him add, edit, and delete them from the bar.

It is a private, single-user Google OAuth app. Only its owner uses it, for his own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

The widget is tmn73/omarchy-calendar.
